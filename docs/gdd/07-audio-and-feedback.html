07 Audio And Feedback

Status: partial

Spec

Presentation should feel arcade complete: mechanical impacts, flipper snaps, bumper hits, plunger tension, lamp responses, DMD messages, score ticks, and a restrained music bed that supports the classic table mood.

Audio context lifecycle

The game uses Vibekit audio context helpers to create and resume Web Audio from the first user gesture. Audio must no-op safely when unavailable.

Mechanical sounds

Flippers, slingshots, bumpers, rollovers, targets, plunger, ball lock, drain, trough serve, tilt warning, and tilt all have distinct audio cues.

Music bed

The table can use a subtle arcade hall or league-night music bed. It must duck or stay out of the way during mechanical event bursts.

Lamp feedback

Lamps and inserts show current objectives, completed lanes, target progress, lock qualification, multiball, jackpots, warnings, and bonus count.

DMD messages

The DMD announces start, skill shot, awards, lock progress, multiball, jackpot, tilt warning, tilt, bonus count, game over, and leaderboard eligibility.

Hit feedback

Major hits combine sound, lamp flash, score popup or DMD update, and optional table vibration or camera impulse. Feedback must not make ball tracking harder.

Settings

Players can adjust or mute music and sound effects. Settings persist locally through Vibekit storage helpers.

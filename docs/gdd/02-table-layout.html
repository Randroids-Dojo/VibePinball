02 Table Layout

Status: partial

Spec

Silverball Social is a single classic physical playfield with enough devices to support a simple ruleset, a skill shot, intentional aiming, and one lock-and-multiball centerpiece. Layout choices must favor readability from the fixed cabinet camera.

Playfield boundaries

The table has a sloped playfield, side walls, an apron, drain, two main flippers, inlanes, outlanes, a shooter lane, and a ball trough. Boundaries must be represented in both render geometry and physics collision.

Lower playfield

The lower third contains left and right flippers, slingshots, inlanes, outlanes, lane rollover sensors, and a central drain. The player should have meaningful save opportunities without making drains feel random or unfair.

Upper playfield

The upper third contains a bumper nest, rollover lanes, a skill-shot lane, and one orbit or return path that can feed the ball back toward the flippers.

Ramps and orbits

The table includes at least one primary ramp and one orbit path. These shots are intentionally aimable from the flippers and support combo scoring without requiring deep modern rules.

Target bank

A bank of physical standup or drop targets advances lock progress. Targets must show clear hit, lit, and completed states.

Lock device

The lock device captures balls for multiball. It can be a saucer, scoop, or physical lane, but it must be readable, reachable, and visibly hold locked balls or represent them on the playfield.

Cabinet props

The scene includes a cabinet, rails, glass impression or rim, plunger housing, speaker panel or DMD area, and decorative arcade hall context. Props cannot block the ball or hide scoring information.

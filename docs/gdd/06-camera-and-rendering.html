06 Camera And Rendering

Status: partial

Spec

The game renders a full 3D pinball cabinet with a fixed tilted playfield camera. The camera must keep the ball, flippers, shooter lane, major shots, and DMD/HUD readable during normal play.

Fixed cabinet camera

The default camera is a stable angled view showing the whole playfield. It does not chase the ball during active play.

Responsive framing

The table remains framed on desktop, tablet, and phone viewports. Mobile may letterbox or scale the playfield, but the ball and flippers must remain readable.

Three.js scene

Three.js owns the visible cabinet, playfield, ball, devices, lamps, DMD plane or overlay, and arcade hall context. Render objects follow physics transforms where applicable.

Materials and lighting

The table uses classic physical materials: wood, metal rails, rubber, plastics, lamps, painted playfield art, and glossy ball reflections. Lighting must not obscure the ball.

Ball readability

The ball uses contrast, highlight, shadow, and optional subtle trail or glint treatment so it is visible against the playfield during high-speed motion.

HUD and DMD

The game shows score, ball number, tilt warnings, lock count, multiball state, and key awards through a DMD-style display or tight HUD that fits the cabinet presentation.

Render performance

The first release should target smooth play on common desktop browsers and acceptable play on modern mobile devices. Visual effects must have budget controls or graceful fallback.

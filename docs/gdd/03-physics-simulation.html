03 Physics Simulation

Status: partial

Spec

Rapier 3D is the authoritative physics engine for v1. The simulation should be strict enough that repeated shots behave consistently, while still allowing table tuning for fair 3-5 minute games.

Rapier world

The game creates a fixed-step Rapier world with stable gravity along the table slope. The render loop may interpolate, but gameplay rules read from the fixed simulation state.

Ball body

The pinball is a dynamic rigid body with spherical collision, material tuning, spin, restitution, friction, and velocity caps that prevent tunneling and unreadable speeds.

Flipper actuation

Flippers are physical bodies or joint-driven devices that transfer force to the ball. They must support held, released, and rapid-tap states without clipping through the ball.

Colliders and sensors

Walls, posts, rubbers, lanes, rollover switches, drain, targets, saucers, ramps, and bumper zones are represented with explicit colliders or sensors. Sensors must not apply physical force unless the device requires it.

Bumpers and slings

Bumpers and slingshots apply impulse in readable directions and trigger audio, lamps, scoring, and device state changes from one collision event path.

Plunger physics

The plunger maps analog charge to launch impulse. The launch must allow a real skill-shot range rather than a single fixed strength.

Nudge and tilt

Nudging applies table-relative impulses to the ball or world. Repeated or excessive nudges raise tilt warnings, and crossing the tilt threshold disables flippers until the current ball drains.

Drain and trough

The drain sensor detects ball loss, routes balls into the trough, starts bonus count, and serves the next ball or ends the game when the three-ball session is complete.

Multiball physics

The simulation supports multiple active balls with independent rigid bodies, sensors, scoring ownership, and drain accounting. Multiball cannot rely on fake overlay balls.
